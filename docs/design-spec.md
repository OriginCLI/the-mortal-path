## At a glance

- **Voice & mood:** Second person, Bittersweet (39), Standard prose, Translated English terms
- **World:** Korean murim conventions; factions: Orthodox sects, Five Great Families, Unorthodox factions, Demonic Cult, Imperial Court, Merchant guilds
- **Cultivation:** Murim ranks (Early / Mid / Late), pace Measured (45), risk Tense (48)
- **Combat:** Technique menu, 8 exchanges, lethality Dangerous (57)
- **Regression:** Every death → back to age 0; carries Timeline knowledge, Secrets & locations, Martial theory, Knowledge of people, Recipes & crafts
- **Bonds:** Full romance; top bonds Master & disciple > Family legacy > Rivalries
- **Events:** 5 per year, Sagas (100) chaining; top Daily life > Fortuitous encounters (기연) > Sect life & politics
- **Look:** Ink & Parchment, Phone-first column, Classical serif
- **Sound:** Adaptive music in Yu mode at 76 BPM
- **Difficulty:** Fair (40), Fail forward
- **First playable:** Age-up loop & life log, Data-driven events, Cultivation & breakthroughs, Combat, Regression & memory journal, Relationships, Sects & reputation, Seclusion time-skips, Save / export / import, Gangho travel / map, Music & sound, Code-drawn art polish; 400 events

## 1. Tone & Writing Style: *The Brush and the Ink*

_Sealed_

- **Narrative voice:** Second person: "You were born under a falling star."
- **Mood:** 39/100 · Bittersweet _(0 = Grim & brutal, 100 = Hopeful & warm)_
- **Prose density:** Standard: Two or three sentences with one vivid detail.
- **Humor:** 53/100 · Wry asides _(0 = Deadly serious, 100 = Comedic)_
- **Terminology:** Translated English: Martial world, senior brother, master, Mount Hua.
- **Life log format:** Chronicle: Year names from the sixty-year cycle, like a historian’s record.
- **Content rating:** Teen: Violence is sharp but not lingering. Romance fades to black.
- **Genre tropes to lean into:** Arrogant young masters, Hidden experts, Fortuitous encounters, Face & honor, Revenge, Hypocritical orthodoxy, Tournament arcs, Regressor knowingness
- **Rank what the writing should deliver:**
  1. Martial spectacle
  2. Wit
  3. Emotional weight
  4. Mystery & foreshadowing
  5. Brevity & readability

## 3. Cultivation & Realms: *The Dantian Awakens*

_Sealed_

- **Realm ladder:** Murim ranks: Third-Rate → Second → First → Peak → Transcendent → Harmony → Mystic → Life & Death.
- **Sub-stages within a realm:** Early / Mid / Late: Three steps per realm; the last is a bottleneck.
- **Pace of cultivation:** 45/100 · Measured _(0 = A lifetime to reach Peak, 100 = Prodigy in a decade)_
- **Breakthrough danger:** 48/100 · Tense _(0 = Safe, 100 = Every attempt could kill)_
- **Qi deviation outcomes:** Internal injury, Crippled meridians, Madness, Death, Twisted insight
- **Sources of progress:** Martial manuals, Elixirs & herbs, Masters, Seclusion, Enlightenment, Qi absorption
- **Innate talent:** Rare constitutions: Nine Yin Meridians, Heavenly Body… Rolled at birth, some cursed.
- **Cultivation paths available:** Orthodox internal arts, Unorthodox shortcuts, Demonic arts, External body arts, Medicine & poison
- **Seclusion controls:** Any length: Pick 1 to 30 years.
- **Rank what cultivation should feel like:**
  1. Breakthrough tension
  2. Knowledge pays off
  3. Path choice
  4. Seclusion time-skips
  5. Steady growth

- **Lethality:** 57/100 · Dangerous _(0 = Bruises & lessons, 100 = Every duel may be your last)_
- **Information shown:** Health & qi bars: Bars without numbers.
- **Technique names:** Poetic: “Plum Blossom Sword: Twenty-Four Falling Petals.”
- **How often fights happen:** 40/100 · Regular _(0 = Rare & meaningful, 100 = Constant)_
- **Kinds of fights:** Honor duels, Ambushes, Tournaments, Sparring, Sect wars, Assassinations, Beasts
- **Ways out of a fight:** Flee, Yield, Talk it down, Dirty tricks, Foreknowledge
- **Rank what combat should be:**
  1. Real stakes
  2. Cinematic text
  3. Tactical depth
  4. Readable outcomes
  5. Quick to resolve

## 5. Regression Rules: *The Wheel Turns Back*

_Sealed_

- **When does regression happen?:** Every death: Die in any way and you wake as a child.
- **Age you return to:** 0 years old _(range 0–20: Infancy → Young adult)_
- **Return point:** Always the same day: A familiar morning, every time.
- **What carries over:** Timeline knowledge, Secrets & locations, Martial theory, Knowledge of people, Recipes & crafts
- **How fixed is history?:** 25/100 · Mostly fixed _(0 = Fixed timeline, 100 = Chaotic)_
- **How much you can change it:** 65/100 · Big change _(0 = Fate resists you, 100 = You rewrite history)_
- **Do others remember?:** Another regressor: Someone else is also turning the wheel.
- **Costs of regression:** Memories fade, Karmic burden, Heaven notices
- **Memory journal:** Codex + personal notes: Auto entries, plus your own annotations.
- **Is there an end?:** Endless wheel: Play forever; no final ending.
